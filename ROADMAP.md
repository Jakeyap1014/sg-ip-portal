# SG IP Portal

## Objective
Enhance and preserve the internal Singapore IP holding-company portal for The Little Company, including the grant map, org-structure page, and landing page.

## Current Status
- Portal files exist in this project:
  - `index.html`
  - `grants.html`
  - `org-chart.html`
  - `TASK.md`
- The build brief and source/link requirements are preserved in `TASK.md`.
- The work originated from yk's `#sg-ip` request on 2026-04-07.

## Durable Notes
- The intended structure shown in the portal is: The Little Company as parent HoldCo, with SG IP Co, SG Ops Co, AU Ops Co, US Ops Co, UK Ops Co, CA Ops Co, and NZ Ops Co.
- SG IP Co is positioned as the new IP holding company, licensing IP to operating companies and receiving royalties.
- Important compliance themes captured in the brief: arm's-length transfer pricing, Singapore economic substance, AU CFC rules, and Pillar Two/global minimum-tax considerations for very large groups.
