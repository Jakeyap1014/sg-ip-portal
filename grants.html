400%
Tax deduction on qualifying IP registration costs (first S$400K/YA)
250%
Enhanced deduction on R&D staff costs & consumables in Singapore
S$20K
Max annual cash payout (20% conversion on up to S$100K qualifying spend)
  • R&D must be performed in Singapore — qualifying staff costs and consumables only

Market Readiness Assistance (MRA)
● High Fit 🆕 Added
Enterprise Singapore · Business Grants Portal
Up to S$100,000 per company per new market · 50% support (enhanced to 70% from 1 Apr 2026)

Co-funds overseas market entry: IP registration in target countries, market research, business matching, and market setup. Particularly relevant for filing trademarks and patents in AU, US, UK markets. Available until EDGE launches H2 2026.

Research & Innovation Scheme for Companies (RISC)
● High Fit
Economic Development Board (EDB)
60% co-funding on local R&D manpower · 30% on foreign manpower & equipment · 3-year qualifying period

Directly funds expansion of R&D and innovation teams in Singapore. Covers basic monthly salaries of qualifying R&D staff (min. S$5,000/month, capped at S$20,000 for support), equipment purchases, and lease costs. Project must develop new or significantly improved products or processes.

Tax Incentives
IP Development Incentive (IDI)
● High Fit
Economic Development Board (EDB) · Apply directly
Reduced tax rate of 5%, 10%, or 15% on qualifying IP income · Incentive period up to 10 years

The flagship tax incentive for IP holding companies in Singapore. Grants a concessionary corporate tax rate — down to 5% — on qualifying IP income (royalties, licensing fees, IP commercialisation gains) via the OECD-compliant modified nexus approach. Rewards R&D performed in Singapore. Elections are irrevocable.

Startup Tax Exemption (SUTE)
● High Fit 🆕 Added
IRAS · Automatic — claim in tax return
75% exemption on first S$100K + 50% exemption on next S$100K · First 3 YAs

Substantial tax savings in the critical early years. No application required — automatically available to qualifying new companies in their first three Years of Assessment. ⚠️ Not available if the company is purely investment holding — must have operational substance.

Writing-Down Allowances (WDA) for IP
● High Fit 🆕 Added
IRAS · Claim in tax return
Deduct capital cost of IP acquisition over 5, 10, or 15 years (straight-line)

Claim allowances on capital spent to acquire IP rights — patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, and registered designs. Effectively lets you deduct the cost of transferring existing group IP into the SG entity over the chosen period. Critical for the IP transfer from AU to SG.

Refundable Investment Credit (RIC)
◐ Medium Fit
EDB / Enterprise Singapore · Approval-basis · 10-year qualifying period
Up to 50% support on each qualifying expenditure category · Unused credits refunded in cash within 4 years

Budget 2024 introduction. Designed to be Pillar Two-compatible (QRTC status). Credits offset corporate income tax; unused portions refunded in cash. Covers R&D, IP, and innovation-related capex. Requires sizeable investment commitment — negotiated with EDB/EnterpriseSG.

Eligibility Checklist
✅Incorporated as Pte. Ltd. in Singapore via ACRA
✅≥ 30% local equity held by Singapore Citizens or PRs — required for most ESG grants
⚠️At least 3 full-time local employees earning ≥ S$1,400/month (required for EIS cash payout)
✅Company age < 10 years at time of application (Startup SG Tech requirement)
⚠️Paid-up capital ≥ 10% of POC quantum or 20% of POV quantum — target S$160K for full POV
✅R&D activities conducted primarily in Singapore (for enhanced deduction rates)
⚠️Technology must be proprietary, novel, and not yet commercially launched before application
✅Apply before project commencement — grants cannot be backdated
Next Steps
1

Incorporate SG IP Co via ACRA BizFile+

Register as a Pte. Ltd. Ensure ≥ 30% local shareholding, appoint a Singapore-resident director, and set paid-up capital at S$160K to unlock full POV grant quantum.

2

Register IP assets with IPOS

File patents, trademarks, and designs in Singapore immediately — EIS deductions accrue from the date of expenditure, so early filing maximises the 400% deduction window.

3

Submit EDG on Business Grants Portal

Apply for EDG to subsidise IP strategy consultancy and product development costs (50%). No minimum spend. Quick turnaround for straightforward applications.

4

Register Interest — Startup SG Tech POC via go.gov.sg/helloesg

Submit an ROI. EnterpriseSG responds within 2 weeks. Aim for POC first — faster approval, lower capital requirement.

5

Apply for IDI with EDB

The crown jewel for an IP Co. Negotiate the IDI award directly with EDB. Nexus ratio rewards SG-based R&D — hire qualifying R&D staff locally from the start. Elections are irrevocable.

6

Submit RISC application to EDB

RISC co-funds 60% of local R&D staff salaries (S$5K–S$20K/month) over a 3-year qualifying period. Apply with a clearly defined R&D project scope.

7

Watch for RIE2030 IAF-ICP Open Call (2026)

RIE2025 closed. A*STAR expected to publish RIE2030 details in 2026. Register interest with an A*STAR Business Development Manager now.
