→ IP Licences Out

SG IP Co grants each Ops Co a licence to use group IP (brand names, product designs, patents, software). Each Ops Co signs an intercompany licensing agreement with arm's-length royalty terms.

← Royalty Income In

Ops Cos pay royalties to SG IP Co for IP use. These payments become tax-deductible expenses in AU/US/UK/CA/NZ, while the income is taxed at the concessionary SG rate (5–10% via IDI) instead of AU's 25–30%.
Important Compliance Considerations
Critical

Transfer Pricing

Royalty rates between SG IP Co and Ops Cos must be at arm's-length. IRAS actively scrutinises related-party transactions. Engage a transfer pricing specialist to set defensible rates and prepare contemporaneous documentation.

Critical

AU CFC Rules

Australia's Controlled Foreign Company (CFC) rules may attribute SG IP Co income back to Australian tax if not structured properly. The CFC regime targets passive income (like royalties) in low-tax jurisdictions. Specialist cross-border tax advice is essential.

High

Economic Substance

Post-BEPS 2.0, Singapore authorities expect real economic substance — actual employees, activities, and decision-making in SG. A brass-plate entity with no operations will not qualify for incentives and risks challenge by both IRAS and foreign tax authorities.

Monitor

OECD Pillar Two (from FY2027)

The 15% global minimum effective tax rate applies to multinational groups with €750M+ consolidated revenue. Likely not an immediate concern for Lifely, but worth monitoring as the group scales. The Refundable Investment Credit (RIC) is specifically designed to be Pillar Two-compatible.

About SG IP Co
Strategic Rationale

Why a dedicated IP entity in Singapore?

Singapore's IP regime offers one of Asia's most competitive environments for intellectual property holding — combining generous tax incentives, deep grant access, and a trusted legal framework. By incorporating SG IP Co as a separate entity, the group can consolidate IP ownership, maximise available deductions, and access Enterprise Singapore's full grant ecosystem as a local company.

Explore available grants →
  • Up to 5% effective tax rate on qualifying IP income under the IP Development Incentive (IDI)
  • 400% deduction on IP registration costs under Enterprise Innovation Scheme (EIS), extended to YA 2028
  • Access to Startup SG Tech POC/POV grants — up to S$1.2M in non-dilutive R&D funding
  • Leverage AU, UK, CA Ops Co for bilateral R&D collaboration under EDG Co-Innovation Programme
  • A*STAR / NUS partnership opportunities via IAF-ICP for deeper innovation alignment

IP Strategy

Recommended IP Transfer

All group IP — patents, trademarks, proprietary software, and trade secrets — should ideally be assigned to SG IP Co post-incorporation. Royalty arrangements with Ops Cos then flow income back to IP Co, where it benefits from Singapore's preferential tax treatment.

Tip: File patents and trademarks in Singapore immediately upon incorporation via IPOS — deductions under EIS accrue from the date of qualifying expenditure, not from approval.
