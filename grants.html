Enterprise Innovation Scheme (EIS)

IRAS · Applicable from YA 2024 to YA 2028 · Claim via annual tax return

400%
Tax deduction on qualifying IP registration costs (first S$400K/YA)
250%
Enhanced deduction on R&D staff costs & consumables in Singapore
S$20K
Max annual cash payout (20% conversion on up to S$100K qualifying spend)
  • R&D must be performed in Singapore — qualifying staff costs and consumables only
  • Company must be SG-registered with min. 3 local employees earning ≥ S$1,400/month
  • Additional 300% deduction on first S$400K R&D expenditure under EIS (on top of base 100%)
  • Cash payout option useful for pre-revenue IP Co — 20% conversion rate, capped at S$20K/YA
  • IP registration deduction covers patents, trademarks, and registered designs filed in SG
  • Apply before project commencement — deductions cannot be backdated beyond YA of spend

Covers third-party consultancy, software, equipment and internal manpower costs. Ideal for IP strategy projects, innovation capability building, and product development. No maximum quantum per project — eligible as soon as SG IP Co is registered.

Research & Innovation Scheme for Companies (RISC)
● High Fit 🆕 Newly Found
Economic Development Board (EDB)
60% co-funding on local R&D manpower · 30% on foreign manpower & equipment · 3-year qualifying period

Directly funds expansion of R&D and innovation teams in Singapore. Covers basic monthly salaries of qualifying R&D staff (min. S$5,000/month, capped at S$20,000 for support), equipment purchases, and lease costs. Project must develop new or significantly improved products or processes — routine changes and adoption projects are excluded. One RISC grant per company per qualifying period; disbursed via annual audited reimbursement claims.

60%local manpower subsidy
IP Development Incentive (IDI)
● High Fit 🆕 Newly Found
Economic Development Board (EDB) · Apply via EDB directly
Reduced tax rate of 5%, 10%, or 15% on qualifying IP income · Incentive period up to 10 years (rate increases 0.5%/year thereafter)

The flagship tax incentive for IP holding companies in Singapore. IDI grants a concessionary corporate tax rate — down to 5% — on a percentage of qualifying IP income (royalties, licensing fees, gains from IP commercialisation) determined by the OECD-compliant modified nexus approach. The nexus ratio rewards companies that perform R&D directly in Singapore. Elections into IDI are irrevocable and can be made standalone or bundled with EDB's Development & Expansion Incentive. With OECD Pillar Two's 15% global minimum tax now in effect, the 15% tier is most practical for large MNE groups; smaller entities can still access 5–10% tiers.

5%min. effective tax on IP income
Refundable Investment Credit (RIC)
◐ Medium Fit 🆕 Newly Found
EDB / Enterprise Singapore · Approval-basis · 10-year qualifying period
Up to 50% support on each qualifying expenditure category · Unused credits refunded in cash within 4 years

Introduced in Budget 2024, the RIC is Singapore's answer to global investment incentive competition post-OECD Pillar Two. Unlike traditional tax deductions, the RIC does not reduce the GloBE effective tax rate — making it structurally attractive for Pillar Two-affected groups. Credits offset corporate income tax payable; any unutilised portion is refunded in cash. Qualifying expenditure categories include R&D, IP, and innovation-related capex. Requires sizeable investment commitment and is awarded on a negotiated, approval basis through EDB or EnterpriseSG.

50%credit on qualifying capex
Recommended Grant Sequencing
Phase 1 · Year 1
Foundation Grants
  • EIS — claim 400% IP registration deductions immediately at incorporation
  • EIS Cash Payout — up to S$20K/YA (attractive if pre-profit)
  • EDG — IP strategy & product development consultancy (50% subsidy)
  • IDI — apply to EDB for 5–10% concessionary tax rate on qualifying IP income
  • RISC — EDB co-funding of 60% on local R&D staff salaries from day one
Low barrier. File EIS via IRAS at year-end. Submit EDG on BGP. Engage EDB directly for IDI and RISC applications.
Phase 2 · Year 1–2
Innovation Grants
  • Startup SG Tech POC — up to S$400K for technical validation
  • EDG CIP — bilateral R&D collaboration leveraging AU / UK / CA Ops Co
Submit ROI at go.gov.sg/helloesg. EnterpriseSG responds within 2 weeks. ~2–3 month evaluation cycle.
Phase 3 · Year 2–4
Scale Grants
  • Startup SG Tech POV — up to S$800K for prototype + commercialisation
  • IAF-ICP — partner with A*STAR/NUS for deep R&D (IP Co as industry partner)
  • RIC — negotiate Refundable Investment Credit with EDB once R&D capex is sizable
Requires proof of POC completion and a reference customer pipeline. RIC is negotiated directly with EDB based on investment commitment.
Eligibility Checklist
✅ Incorporated as private limited company (Pte. Ltd.) in Singapore via ACRA
✅ ≥ 30% local equity held by Singapore Citizens or PRs — required for most ESG grants
⚠️ At least 3 full-time local employees earning ≥ S$1,400/month (required for EIS cash payout)
✅ Company age < 10 years at time of application (Startup SG Tech requirement)
⚠️ Paid-up capital ≥ 10% of POC quantum or 20% of POV quantum — target S$160K to unlock full POV
✅ R&D activities conducted primarily in Singapore (for enhanced in-Singapore deduction rates)
⚠️ Technology must be proprietary, novel, and not yet commercially launched before application
✅ Apply before project commencement — grants cannot be backdated
Next Steps
1

Incorporate SG IP Co via ACRA

Register as a Pte. Ltd. Ensure ≥ 30% local shareholding, appoint a Singapore-resident director, and set paid-up capital at S$160K to unlock full POV grant quantum.

2

Register IP assets with IPOS

File patents, trademarks, and designs in Singapore immediately — EIS deductions accrue from the date of expenditure, so early filing maximises the 400% deduction window.

3

Submit EDG on Business Grants Portal (BGP)

Apply for EDG to subsidise IP strategy consultancy and product development costs (50%). No minimum spend required; quick turnaround for straightforward applications.

4

Register Interest — Startup SG Tech POC

Submit a Registration of Interest (ROI) at go.gov.sg/helloesg. EnterpriseSG responds within 2 weeks with an indicative assessment. Aim for POC first — faster approval, lower capital requirement.

5

Apply for IDI with EDB — target 5% concessionary rate on IP income

The IP Development Incentive is the crown jewel for an IP Co. Engage EDB directly to negotiate the IDI award. The nexus ratio rewards R&D performed in Singapore, so ensure qualifying R&D staff are hired locally from the start. Elections are irrevocable — structure IP assets carefully before election.

6

Submit RISC application to EDB for R&D manpower co-funding

RISC co-funds 60% of local R&D staff salaries (S$5K–S$20K/month) over a 3-year qualifying period. Ideal for building out a core Singapore-based R&D team. Apply to EDB with a clearly defined R&D project scope; routine product adoption does not qualify.

7

Watch for RIE2030 IAF-ICP Open Call (Q1–Q2 2026)

The RIE2025 call has closed. A*STAR is expected to publish RIE2030 IAF-ICP details between January–March 2026. Register interest with an A*STAR Business Development Manager now to be first in queue.
